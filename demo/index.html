every shadow on this page is one css line: box-shadow: var(--rs-x) var(--rs-y) var(--rs-blur) rgba(0,0,0,var(--rs-alpha)). the library computes where the sun — or at night, the moon — actually is for the chosen place and time, and writes those four variables. the page tint follows data-sky.
